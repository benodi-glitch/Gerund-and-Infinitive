Quick Reference

A gerund and an infinitive can both refer to actions, but English verbs conventionally select different patterns.

Verb + gerund: enjoy working · avoid spending · suggest changing

Verb + infinitive: decide to leave · plan to expand · agree to cooperate

Preposition + gerund: interested in learning · responsible for managing · committed to improving

Watch the “to” trap: decide to expand ≠ committed to expanding. In the first, to introduces an infinitive; in the second, to is a preposition.
